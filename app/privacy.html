Privacy policy

What the BTM Pizza app collects, why, who sees it and how to delete it.

1. Who we are

  • The BTM Pizza app and Oven Club are run by Manjunatha Foods, the legal entity behind BTM Pizza, Liss Arcade, 16th Main Rd, BTM Layout 2nd Stage, Bengaluru ("we", "us"). We decide how your personal data is used and are responsible for it under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • The app is published on the App Store under the name of Karthik Nayak, a partner in Manjunatha Foods. Your data is handled by Manjunatha Foods as this policy describes.
  • This policy covers the app and the loyalty programme. It applies together with our Terms & conditions.

2. What we collect

  • Your name and Indian mobile number, which you give us when you sign up and sign in.
  • An account ID and your referral code, which we create for your account.
  • Your loyalty activity: the bills you scan (bill number and amount), Dough Coins earned and redeemed, your visits and tier, and the offers you open and use at the counter.
  • Referrals: whose code you joined with, and who joined with yours.
  • If you allow notifications: your phone's push notification token, with the app's version, so we can send them.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, browsing, or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. The camera is used only to read the counter QR code on your phone: no picture is stored or sent, only the code's text.

3. How we use it

  • To sign you in, run your account, add and redeem coins, count visits for tiers, apply offers and pay referral rewards.
  • To send notifications about your coins, and, only if you agreed on the consent screen or turned them on in Profile, offers and pizza news.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse of the programme, answer your questions, and keep records our accounts and the law require.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising on other services, or build profiles for anyone else.

4. Who we share it with

  • SMS provider: to text you your one-time sign-in code, we pass your mobile number and the code to our SMS provider (Fast2SMS, India). They use it only to deliver the message.
  • Apple: notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service, which receives your push token and the notification's text.
  • Your referrer: if you joined with a friend's code, that friend sees your first name, when you joined and the coins their share earned from your bills. Nothing else, such as your number or bills, is shown.
  • When you show an offer's QR code at the counter, our staff see that offer, when it is valid and your tier. They do not see your mobile number or coin balance.
  • Authorities, only when Indian law requires it. Your data is stored on our own servers in India.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account data is kept while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account, your name, number, referral links, push tokens, offers and coin history are deleted. Bill records are kept without your details, because our accounts need them.
  • Sign-in codes expire within minutes. Push tokens are removed when you log out or turn notifications off. Technical logs are kept only for a short time, for security and troubleshooting.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Notifications: turn them off any time in Profile ("Notifications") or in iOS Settings. Off removes this phone from our notification list.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile, then Delete account. It takes effect at once.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or erase your data, or withdraw a consent you gave, by messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 88678 86778. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before.
  • If we do not resolve a complaint, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Children

  • The programme is for people aged 18 or over, or younger people using the app with a parent's or guardian's consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children without that consent.

8. Changes and contact

  • We will announce material changes to this policy in the app, with the new date above.
  • Questions or grievances: message us on WhatsApp at +91 88678 86778, or speak to the team at BTM Pizza, Liss Arcade, 16th Main Rd, BTM Layout 2nd Stage, Bengaluru. We aim to reply within 48 hours and resolve complaints within 30 days.
