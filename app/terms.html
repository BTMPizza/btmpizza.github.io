Dough Coins

  • Coins are earned on eligible bills at BTM Pizza by scanning the bill's QR code in the app, at the rate shown in the app at the time.

  • Coins may expire if your account is inactive or as otherwise announced in the app. We will give notice in the app before any change to expiry.
  • If a bill is cancelled, refunded or reversed, the coins earned on it are removed, even if that leaves your balance below zero.
