Common questions

How do Dough Coins and Oven Club tiers work?

You earn Dough Coins automatically on eligible orders placed via the BTM Pizza app. Open the Oven Club / Rewards tab in the app to check your current tier, coin balance, and active offers.

I did not receive my login OTP

Double-check that your 10-digit Indian mobile number (+91) is entered correctly and that SMS delivery is not blocked. If the OTP still does not arrive, message us on WhatsApp or call +91 88678 86778.

My referral reward or order points are missing

Reach out via WhatsApp or email with your registered phone number and order receipt or bill details. Our team will review and credit eligible Dough Coins within 24–48 hours.

How do I delete my account or data?

You can delete your account directly from your profile settings inside the app, or email me@karthiknayak.com from your registered details to request complete account deletion. See our Privacy policy for details.
