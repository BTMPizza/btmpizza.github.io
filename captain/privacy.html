Privacy policy

How the Captain app handles staff and customer data, by Manjunatha Foods.

1. Who we are

  • Manjunatha Foods runs BTM Pizza, Liss Arcade, 16th Main Rd, BTM Layout 2nd Stage, Bengaluru ("we", "us"). We decide how the data in the Captain app is used and are responsible for it under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • This policy covers the BTM Captain staff app. The BTM Pizza customer app has its own privacy policy.

2. What we collect about staff

  • Your name, mobile number, account ID (shown as your employee ID) and staff role. The owner enters your name and number when adding you.
  • The loyalty codes you create: bill number, bill amount or coins, time, and your employee ID. When a customer scans a code, it is linked to that customer's account.
  • The offers you redeem: the offer, the time and your employee ID.
  • Your sign-in tokens, kept only in this phone's iOS Keychain until you sign out.

3. Customer data in the app

  • To redeem an offer, the app reads the QR code in the customer's BTM Pizza app. It sends the customer's short-lived sign-in token and offer code to our server to check and redeem that offer. The token is not stored on your phone or shown to you.
  • You see the offer, its expiry, and the customer's tier and offer day. The app does not show customers' names or phone numbers.

4. What we don't collect

  • The camera is used only to read QR codes: the image stays on the phone and only the code's text is used. No photos are taken or stored.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or analytics, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. How we use it

  • To sign you in, record loyalty codes and offer redemptions, keep accounts of the loyalty programme, and prevent and investigate misuse.

6. Who we share it with

  • SMS provider: to text you your one-time sign-in code, we pass your mobile number and the code to Fast2SMS (India). They use it only to deliver the message.
  • Authorities, only when Indian law requires it. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising. Data is stored on our own servers in India.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your staff account is kept while you work with us. When your staff access ends, your role is removed. On request we also delete your account, as long as no legal or accounting duty needs us to keep it.
  • Records of the codes and offers you handled are kept, with your employee ID, for as long as our accounts and fraud checks need them. Sign-in codes expire within minutes.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask to see, correct or erase your data, or to have your staff access removed, from the account menu ("Remove my access and data") or on WhatsApp at +91 88678 86778.
  • If we do not resolve a complaint, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Changes and contact

  • We will show material changes to this policy in the app, with the new date below.
  • Questions or grievances: WhatsApp +91 88678 86778, or speak to the owner at BTM Pizza. We aim to reply within 48 hours and resolve complaints within 30 days.
