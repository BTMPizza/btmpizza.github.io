Staff terms of use

For BTM Pizza staff using the Captain app, run by Manjunatha Foods, Bengaluru.

1. About the app

  • BTM Captain is the counter app for the staff of BTM Pizza, Liss Arcade, 16th Main Rd, BTM Layout 2nd Stage, Bengaluru. It is operated by Manjunatha Foods ("we", "us").
  • Only people the owner has added as staff (captains and owners) can sign in. The app does not create accounts. Customers use the separate BTM Pizza app.
  • By signing in you agree to these terms and to our privacy policy.

2. Your staff account

  • Your account is tied to the mobile number the owner added and is confirmed by a one-time code. Keep your phone and codes to yourself and do not let anyone else use your account.
  • Every loyalty code and offer you handle is recorded under your employee ID. Activity on your account is treated as yours until you tell us it was misused.

3. Loyalty codes and offers

  • Create earn and redeem codes only for real bills at BTM Pizza, with the correct bill number, amount and coins. One bill gets one loyalty transaction.
  • Never create codes for yourself, your friends or anyone who did not pay the bill, and never share codes outside the counter.
  • Redeem an offer only from the customer's own BTM Pizza app, shown to you at the counter, and only for that customer's bill.
  • Dough Coins are loyalty rewards with no cash value. The customer programme is covered by the Oven Club terms in the BTM Pizza app.

4. Misuse

  • We may suspend or remove your access, and cancel or reverse coins, codes or offers, if we find or reasonably suspect misuse, fraud or mistakes. This does not limit any other rights we or you have under your employment.

5. Ending access

  • The owner can remove your staff access at any time, for example when you leave. You can ask for your access and data to be removed from the account menu ("Remove my access and data").

6. The service

  • The app is provided "as is" for work at BTM Pizza. We try to keep it available and accurate but do not guarantee uninterrupted service. We may change the app or these terms; material changes will be shown in the app.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Bengaluru, Karnataka have jurisdiction.

7. Contact

  • Questions or problems: use "Help and contact" in the account menu, message us on WhatsApp at +91 88678 86778, or speak to the owner at the outlet.
